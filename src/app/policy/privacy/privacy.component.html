Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 4, 2026

Thank you for using GymTracker.

Your privacy is important. This Privacy Policy explains what information is collected, how it is used, and how it is protected.

Information We Collect

When you create an account, we collect:

  • Email address
  • Encrypted (hashed) password

While using the application, we store:

  • Workout logs
  • Workout routines
  • Exercise history
  • Any information you choose to enter into the application.

How Your Information Is Used

Your information is used only to:

  • Authenticate your account
  • Save your workouts and routines
  • Display your workout history
  • Improve the reliability and functionality of the application

Your personal information is never sold, rented, or shared with advertisers.

Data Storage

The application is hosted using trusted cloud services.

Your data is securely stored in a PostgreSQL database hosted by Neon.

The frontend is hosted on Vercel and the backend is hosted on Render.

Third-Party Services

The application relies on the following providers to operate:

  • Vercel (Frontend Hosting)
  • Render (Backend Hosting)
  • Neon (PostgreSQL Database)

These providers may process technical information required to deliver their services.

GymTracker does not use advertising services, analytics platforms, or third-party tracking technologies that monitor user behavior.

Password Security

Passwords are never stored in plain text.

Passwords are securely hashed before being stored in the database.

Your Data

You retain ownership of the workout data you create.

If you would like your account and all associated workout data removed, please contact the email address below.

Children's Privacy

GymTracker is not specifically intended for children under the age of 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time. Any changes become effective immediately upon publication on this page.
